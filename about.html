About gameShelf

gameShelf is a browser-based game platform that hosts a curated collection of classic games — all playable directly in your browser with no downloads required. No installations, no plugins, just click and play.

Our platform features seven categories of browser-based games:

  • Action — Fast-paced games like Breakout where you smash bricks and keep the ball bouncing.
  • Puzzle — Brain-teasers including Tetris, 2048, Minesweeper, Tic Tac Toe, and Memory Match.
  • Arcade — Timeless classics like Snake and Pac-Man that never get old.
  • Strategy — Thoughtful games that test your planning and tactics — like Connect Four and chess-based puzzles. Coming soon.
  • Board — Classic board and card games reimagined for the web — like Sliding Tile Puzzle and beyond. Coming soon.
  • Casual — Quick, relaxed games perfect for a short break — like Simon Says.

Every game is built entirely with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — running smoothly on desktop and mobile browsers alike. Whether you're taking a short break or looking for some nostalgic fun, gameShelf has something for you.
